# Changelog

## Unreleased

### More levels
- Minefield, Tone Pads and Floor Is Lava now have 6 levels each (up from 3).
  - **Minefield 4-6:** more mines to remember (up to 7) and less time to study.
  - **Tone Pads 4-6:** longer tunes (up to 11 notes) played faster.
  - **Floor Is Lava 4-6:** shorter flashes, escapes up to two tiles away, and the crumbling floor returns.

### Achievements for every mode
- 15 new achievements, 5 per mode, each on its own tab in the achievements screen:
  - **Minefield:** Safe Crossing, Mine Dodger, Minefield Master, Light Feet, Untouchable.
  - **Tone Pads:** First Tune, In Tune, Maestro, Perfect Pitch, Long Memory.
  - **Floor Is Lava:** Hot Feet, Fire Walker, Lava Lord, Not Even Warm, Survivor.
- Progress in each mode is saved. Existing saves keep their Classic progress and achievements.

### Floor Is Lava
- The warning only flashes, then the board goes dark: remember where the lava will land.
- Tiles about to burn show an amber "!" instead of lava. Lava only appears when it lands.
- No hints on any level.
- The start and between-wave prompts say why you stand in the middle.

## v2.0.0 (2026-09-30)

Three new ways to play, a new menu, and a new look for every mode.

### New game modes
- **Minefield**: a 4x4 board of hidden mines. Memorise where the red mines are, then walk to the blue tile once they hide. Step on a mine or off the board and the round is over. 3 levels, each with more mines and less time to study.
- **Tone Pads**: four coloured pads around a home tile in the middle. A tune plays on a small copy of the board that floats in front of you and follows your view, so you can face any direction. Step the pads back in the same order; each pad plays its own note. 3 levels of longer, faster tunes.
- **Floor Is Lava**: a 4x4 board surrounded by a lava moat. Amber tiles warn where lava is about to land, so get to a safe tile before it does. Level 1 shows you a safe tile. Level 2 drops the hint and allows diagonal escapes. Level 3 needs two-tile escapes, and the floor crumbles behind you. 3 levels.

### Changes
- One life in every new mode: one mistake restarts the level.
- Minefield: a caution-tape border marks the edge of the board, and it turns red as you get close.
- Glowing tile frames replace the flat tiles in every mode, including Classic.
- New main menu: a card for each mode with its icon, its rules in one line and a level count tag.
- Plain white "Memory Grid" title in place of the animated logo.
- Achievements screen has a tab per mode. Classic's achievements are unchanged; the new modes' achievements are coming later.

### Fixes
- Minefield layouts can always be finished without squeezing diagonally between two mines.
- Minefield: you can no longer walk around the outside of the board to skip the mines.
